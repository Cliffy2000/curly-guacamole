### Observations on Moondrop's collab construction

1. **CNY 239 is a recurring anchor price** for virtual-singer gift boxes (Luo Tianyi, Miku),
   both built on existing chassis (Pudding, MIRAGE). Reskin-plus-voice-prompts, not re-tuned.
2. **The virtual-singer collabs carry the partner's own voice prompts** — Luo Tianyi and Miku
   are synthesised voices, so "voice talent" here is a voicebank, not a performer. A distinct
   category from both human seiyuu and VTubers. Flagging for the client's segmentation.
3. **MOEYU appears as an intermediary** on the Miku products — a licensing/merch partner rather
   than the rightsholder (Crypton). Suggests Chinese collabs route through sub-licensors.
4. Moondrop's collab prices (CNY 219–599) sit far below its own premium range (DUSK CNY 2,499),
   i.e. collabs are used for volume and reach, not for margin at the top of the line.

high-margin, high-loyalty niche, not a volume play. State it plainly to the client.

## PART E — THE CHINA HUMAN-TALENT GAP

**This is the most significant hole in the dataset so far, and it may be a real market finding
rather than a research failure.**

On the Japanese side, human-talent collabs are abundant and well documented — Kiramune (13
seiyuu), Universal Music/TOoKA BASE (6 seiyuu), Koiwai Kotori (development lead), Suou Patra
(VTuber, 5 installments).

On the Chinese side, everything confirmed so far is **game IP, anime IP, virtual singers
(voicebanks), or a foreign voice actress** (Moondrop × 安野希世乃, who is Japanese). Not one
confirmed collab with a **named Chinese voice actor as themselves** has been found yet.

Candidate explanations, none yet evidenced — do not assert any of these:
- Chinese CV talent may license through platforms (MissEvan/bilibili) rather than as individuals
- Post-2021 regulatory pressure on fan-economy and idol merchandising may suppress
  individual-talent merchandise
- It may simply exist and not yet be found

**Next actions:** search named Chinese CV talent directly (边江, 阿杰, 姜广涛, 夏磊, 杨天翔,
山新, 季冠霖), plus 广播剧 联名 耳机, plus 摩点 crowdfunding, plus bilibili VUP hardware collabs.
Until then this is recorded as **an open question, not a conclusion**.
