## Confirmed structural findings so far

1. **Made-to-order (完全受注生産) is the dominant JP model** for talent collabs — ONKYO
   states it explicitly. Removes inventory risk from the audio brand. Long order-to-ship
   lead times corroborate: Zeeny Sakuya Azusa announced 2023-04, shipped ~2024-05.
2. **Recurring series indicate viability** — final × Suou Patra reached a 4th installment
   with a simultaneous re-release of the 3rd. AVIOT hololive EN reached a 4th unit (Myth →
   Advent → Promise → Justice). Moondrop 猫饼 platform reused across 3 IPs.
3. **Voice-clip count is the industry's own collab-depth currency** (42 / 200+ / 270+ / 370+),
   and it scales with price tier.
4. **Individual talent prices below IP units** in AVIOT's line (18,700 vs 22,880).
5. **Platform reuse is universal** — one chassis (TE-V1R, AOW04, 猫饼, Zeeny Lights 3)
   re-skinned across many partners. Acoustic change is the exception, not the rule.
   final × Patra and Moondrop's ASMR work are the exceptions that do re-tune.
