## 1. CHINA — headphone market, with price-band structure

### 1.1 Headline (RUNTO 洛图科技, all-channel)

| Metric | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 forecast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unit sales | **218 mn pairs (2.18亿副)**, +7.6% | **203 mn pairs (2.03亿副), −6.9%** | −2.2%, **falling below 200 mn** |
| Sales value | CNY 42.8 bn (428亿元), +9.8% | **CNY 45.2 bn (452亿元), +5.6%** | — |
| Average price | ~CNY 196 (derived) | **CNY 223, +13.3%** | — |

**Volume fell while value rose** — the market is shifting up-price, not growing. That is the single
most important structural fact for a premium-positioned entrant.

H1 2024 reference: 51.15 mn pairs (+6.9%), CNY 11.59 bn (+7.2%); **online = 84% of all-channel
volume**. Market had 6 consecutive quarters of YoY growth from Q1 2023.

⚠ **Mapping to the brief's bands.** The brief asks for 1–100 / 101–300 / 301–512 / 513–1200 /
1201–3000 / 3001–9999 / 10000–20000 / 20000+. RUNTO publishes 0–100 / 100–200 / 200–300 /
300–500 / 500–1000 / 1000–1500 / 1500–2000 / 2000+. So:
- brief 1–100 ← RUNTO 0–100 (direct)

- brief 513–1200 ← RUNTO 500–1000 + part of 1000–1500
- brief 1201–3000 ← RUNTO 1500–2000 + 2000+ partial
- brief 3001+ — **no published Chinese band exists above 2000+**. Nothing to populate.
**The 301–512 and 513–1200 boundaries do not correspond to any published band in any market.**

## 2. JAPAN — market and consumer

### 2.1 Price-band structure (BCN POS, current)
**Under JPY 10,000: ~40% · JPY 10,000–20,000: ~20% · Over JPY 20,000: ~40%.**
Polarisation began around 2020. Channel split **60–70% physical / 30–40% online** —
the inverse of China's 84% online.
Seasonal peaks: **March, July, December**.
⚠ **Only three bands exist in published Japanese data.** The brief's eight bands cannot be
populated for Japan. Earlier BCN figure (Jan 2022) was 46.7% sub-10k and ~30% over-25k, so the
market has polarised further since.
Sources: https://www.bcnretail.com/news/detail/20251129_574920.html (direct fetch 404 previously;
figures from search-surfaced summary) · https://www.bcnretail.com/research/detail/20220131_263891.html

## 4. CHINA AUDIO-DRAMA (广播剧) MARKET — for the seiyuu-adjacent demand pool

⚠ **Severe magnitude problems in the published figures. Recorded but NOT usable as stated.**

Reported series: market grew from **CNY 10.5 bn (2019)** to **CNY 104.4 bn (2024)**, projected
**CNY 185.5 bn by 2028**, with a stated CAGR of **89.5%**.
- 10.5 → 104.4 over five years is ~58% CAGR, **not 89.5%**. The stated CAGR is internally
  inconsistent with its own endpoints.
- 104.4 → 185.5 over four years is ~15% CAGR, also inconsistent with 89.5%.
Reported users: **2.7 billion (2019–2024)**, projected ~3 billion by 2028.
- China's population is ~1.4 billion. **2.7 billion "users" cannot be persons.** Likely cumulative
  user-sessions or a mis-transcription of 2.7亿 (270 million), which would be plausible.

**Treat the whole series as unverified.** The defensible figures nearby are:
- Platform concentration: **Ximalaya, Qingting FM, Lizhi FM, MissEvan ≈ 75–85% combined share**
- **22 audio dramas exceeded 100 million listens** (as of the July 2025 MissEvan content-ecology conference)
- MissEvan's own disclosed metrics (from Q4 work): avg paid user buys **12 dramas/year**; top user
  spend **>CNY 150,000**; 20+ in-house producers, 50+ partner studios
- Per-title economics (from Q2 work): 『魔道祖师』 >CNY 10 mn; 『破云』『默读』 >CNY 1 mn each;
  top-tier CV fee **CNY 5,000–10,000 per episode**
- 『杀破狼』 merchandise crowdfunding **CNY 7.71 mn from ~15,100 backers**, 729声工场
