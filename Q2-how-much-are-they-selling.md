# Q2 — HOW MUCH ARE THEY SELLING

Compiled 2026-09-07. Roughly 220 sources retrieved to date.

---

## 0. THE HONEST ANSWER FIRST

**Per-SKU volume is structurally unobservable for most of this market, and that is a property
of the business model rather than a research failure.**

The dominant Japanese model is **完全受注生産 — fully made-to-order.** An order window opens,
closes, and production follows. There is no inventory, no channel sell-through to measure, no
retail POS trail, and no commercial reason for the brand to publish a number. ONKYO states the
made-to-order model explicitly on its own product pages. AVIOT, Zeeny and Universal Music all
run closed order windows.

So the answer comes in four tiers of decreasing reliability, and I have kept them separate
rather than blending them into a single false estimate:

| Tier | What it is | How many data points |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Disclosed** | brand published a unit or funding figure | **5** |
| **B. Derived** | price × disclosed units, both primary-sourced | **2** |
| **C. Marketplace counters** | platform-published sales counts (China only) | **1, weak sourcing** |
| **D. Proxies** | sell-out speed, restocks, resale volume | several |

**No estimate is modelled from assumption anywhere in this document.** Where a number cannot
be established, it is named as not established.

## 1. TIER A — DISCLOSED FIGURES

### 1.1 The single best per-SKU datapoint in either market

**ag × 周防パトラ — COTSUBU for ASMR Patra Edition -BLACK Ver.-**

| Metric | Value | Source tier |
|---|---|---|
| Orders in first **12 hours** | **10,000+ units** | 1 (final Inc. PR Times) |
| Within-2023 delivery allocation | **25,000 units — SOLD OUT** | 1 / 4 |
| Overflow beyond 25,000 | shipped **April 2024 or later**, cited material procurement | 1 |
| Unit price | **JPY 8,200** incl. tax (+ JPY 780 shipping) | 1 |
| Order window | 2023-08-20 22:00 → 2023-09-08 23:59 | 1 |

This is the only case found where a brand published hard unit numbers for a single collaboration
SKU. Everything else in Japan is made-to-order and silent.

### 1.2 The category anchor — and it is a big one

**final: 300,000 cumulative units of ASMR/sleep-tuned wireless earphones, as of December 2025.**

The footnote definition matters and is quoted here because it determines what the number covers:
「ASMR・寝ホン向けにチューニングを施した自社ワイヤレスイヤホンの累計販売実績」 — cumulative sales
of the company's own wireless earphones tuned for ASMR and sleep listening.

So this figure spans the COTSUBU for ASMR / ZE500 / VR1000 families **including** the Patra
collaborations and the Blue Archive editions, plus non-collab models in the same tuning family.
Source: https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000216.000121822.html (final Inc. official, tier 1)

**DERIVED — a floor on the Patra series' share of that category:**
Known Patra units = 25,000 (2nd installment allocation) + 1,000 (1st installment final restock)
+ 400 (1st installment event allocation) = **26,400 units minimum**.
26,400 ÷ 300,000 = **≥ 8.8% of final's entire ASMR/sleep category**, from one VTuber partnership.
This is a **floor**, not an estimate: installments 3, 4 and 5 are unquantified, and the 1st
installment's total is disputed (see §5). The true share is higher, by an unknown amount.

**1. ag COTSUBU for ASMR Patra Edition -BLACK Ver.-, within-year allocation**

Excludes shipping revenue and the >25,000-unit overflow. Both inputs tier 1.

**2. KPro01**
**JPY 166,070,000** raised (or 160,000,000 — see conflict above). This *is* the revenue figure;
crowdfunding gross is directly disclosed.

**Nothing else in the dataset supports a revenue calculation.** Of 113 product rows,
`units_sold` is populated on 4. That is the whole picture.

## 4. TIER D — PROXIES WHERE VOLUME IS ABSENT

These do not give units. They give **direction and relative intensity**, which is what is
actually available for a made-to-order market.

### 4.1 Sell-out and restock behaviour — the strongest available signal

| Product | Signal |
|---|---|
| ag COTSUBU Patra 1st | sold out; **restocked; restock also sold out**; final states many customers still could not buy; then a declared **final production run** of 1,000 units |
| ag COTSUBU Patra 2nd | **10,000 orders in 12 hours**; 25,000 allocation exhausted; overflow pushed ~4 months |
| final × Patra series | reached a **5th installment**; the **3rd was re-released** alongside the 4th's launch |
| AVIOT hololive EN | reached a **4th unit** (Myth → Advent → Promise → Justice); Advent **SOLD OUT**, Myth sales ended |
| AVIOT Gintama | **4 simultaneous SKUs** for one franchise |
| ONKYO Girls Band Cry | TOGENASHI TOGEARI model put to **re-order** |
| ONKYO 超かぐや姫 | **second order round** (二次受注) |
| Taito/Cafereo Groove Coaster × hololive | vol.1 **restocked on demand**; reached **vol.3** |
| Universal Music オトもラジオ | reached a **3rd installment** |
| Moondrop 猫饼 platform | reused across **3 separate IPs** |

**Interpretive rule I have applied:** a re-order, a restock, or an Nth installment is a
disclosure-free statement by the seller that demand exceeded supply. It is the most reliable
volume-adjacent evidence this market produces.

### 4.2 Secondary market — thin, and that itself is informative

Aucfan data for the query 「コラボイヤホン」: **5 completed auctions in the trailing 30 days,
average winning price JPY 11,883.** Retrieval of the full page returned HTTP 429; figures are
from the search-surfaced summary.
A **dedicated buy-back service for anime/game/VTuber collab earphones** operates at
goods-mate.com, and e☆イヤホン's own category commentary states popular models leave regular
retail after their sales window and **rise sharply in resale value**.

**Reading:** the resale infrastructure exists and specialises, but 5 auctions per month is a
very thin float. For made-to-order goods bought by fans of a specific talent, that is consistent
with **buyers keeping them**. Low resale liquidity is not evidence of low demand here.
Sources: https://aucfan.com/intro/q-~a5b3a5e9a5dca5a4a5e4a5dba5f3/ · https://goods-mate.com/earphones/ ·
https://www.e-earphone.jp/blogs/feature/collaboration_earphone

### 4.3 The collab price premium — one commentary figure, not a measurement
Auction-market commentary characterises collab models as a recolour, print and repackage of an
existing product at **about 20% above the base product**. This is **commentary, not a measured
premium**, and my own SKU data does not cleanly test it because most collab SKUs in the dataset
have no matched base-SKU price. Recorded as a claim to verify, not a finding.

## 5. TOP-DOWN BOUND — the licensing side

This is the one place where an audited public number touches this market.

**COVER Corp (hololive), FY2026/3: Licensing / Tie-up revenue = JPY 7.198 bn, +25.3% YoY.**
Against total revenue of JPY 49.33 bn, that is **14.6%** of the company.

**What it bounds, precisely:** all hololive licensing across every category — games, apparel,
food, collaborations, campaigns — of which collab earphones are one line. So
**JPY 7.198 bn is a hard ceiling on hololive-side licensing income from earphone deals, and the
actual figure is a small and undisclosed fraction of it.** COVER attributes the segment's growth
to more deals and more counterparty companies, i.e. licensing *volume* is rising.

**ANYCOLOR (Nijisanji) gives no equivalent bound.** It books goods inside **commerce (~70% of
JPY 55.681 bn revenue)** and discloses **no separate licensing line**. Nijisanji collab-earphone
income is therefore **not observable at all** from public filings. This is structural, not a
missing datapoint.

**Implication for the client:** the licensor's take on this category is invisible in both
directions. Nobody outside the deals knows what the rate is (`gaps.csv` G001 — no royalty rate
found in ~220 sources), and the aggregate is buried inside a much larger line.

## 6. MARKET DENOMINATORS — so the niche can be sized against something

⚠ These are third-party market-research figures with unresolved unit-magnitude risk and should
be re-read from primaries before publication. Recorded with that caveat.

**Japan**
- TWS reached **35.0% of Japanese headphone/earphone unit sales** — first time above 30% (BCN,
  reported Jan 2022)
- BCN POS price-band split: **sub-JPY 10,000 = 46.7%**; **JPY 25,000+ high-end ≈ 30%**
- Apple's share peaked at **50.0%** by May 2020 and has since declined as Sony, JVC,
  audio-technica and Anker gained
- Japan headphone market **~USD 738.2 mn (2024)**, forecast ~USD 992.1 mn by 2034, **CAGR 3.00%**
- ⚠ Direct fetches of the BCN research page (404) and the smhn.info market article (403) both
  failed; the BCN percentages above come from search-surfaced summaries.

**Collab pricing sits in a specific gap.** Japanese collab SKUs cluster at **JPY 18,000–22,880**,
which is *above* the 46.7% entry band and *below* the JPY 25,000+ high-end band. The ONKYO
Girls Band Cry custom IEMs (JPY 104,500–250,000) are far above the entire consumer distribution.

**China**
- China headphone market **2024: 218 million units (+7.6%), CNY 42.8 bn (+9.8%)** — RUNTO 洛图科技.
  DERIVED implied ASP **CNY 196**, which is the cross-check that rules out a "428 billion yuan" reading.
- Global TWS: USD 13.24 bn (2024) → USD 15.86 bn (2025); Q2 2024 TWS **+12.6% YoY**

**Sizing the niche honestly.** Against a CNY 42.8 bn Chinese headphone market, Moondrop's entire
business — the largest ACG-collab operator in China — is order **CNY 100 mn**, i.e. roughly
**0.2–0.3% of the national market**. This is a high-loyalty, high-attachment niche, **not a
volume play.** That framing should be stated plainly to the client rather than dressed up.

## 8. WHAT THIS MEANS — the answer in one paragraph

The confirmed volume evidence supports **tens of thousands of units per successful collaboration
wave** (ag Patra 25,000; Moondrop Robin ~20,000 on weak sourcing), **hundreds of thousands of
units at brand-category level** (final ASMR/sleep 300,000 cumulative; AVIOT Q-series 250,000),
and **~JPY 200 million of gross order value from a single strong SKU**. Crowdfunding shows a
ceiling of **~JPY 166 million from ~10,000 backers** for a talent-led product. Against national
headphone markets, the niche is a fraction of a percent. The economics work not on volume but on
**attachment**: made-to-order removes inventory risk, order windows convert fandom directly into
committed demand, and repeat installments — five for one VTuber partnership — indicate the
customer base re-buys.
