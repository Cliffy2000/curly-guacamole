### COVER Corp (hololive) — TSE ticker 5253 — VERIFIED

**FY2026/3 full year (Apr 2025 – Mar 2026), non-consolidated:**

| Line | Value | YoY |
|---|---|---|
| **Revenue** | **JPY 49.33 bn** (493億3,000万円) | **+13.7%** |
| **Merchandising** | **JPY 23.747 bn** (237億4,700万円) | **+15.6%** |
| **Licensing / Tie-up** | **JPY 7.198 bn** (71億9,800万円) | **+25.3%** |
| Net income | — | **−45.7%** |

**DERIVED segment mix:** Merchandising = 48.1% of revenue (23.747 ÷ 49.33).
Licensing/Tie-up = 14.6% of revenue (7.198 ÷ 49.33).

**Licensing/Tie-up is the line that carries collab-earphone income for hololive.** It is the
fastest-growing disclosed segment at +25.3%, ahead of merchandising's +15.6%. COVER attributes
its growth to strengthened coordination with **domestic and overseas trading agents** and an
expanded sales organisation, with growth in both **deal count and number of counterparty
companies** — i.e. licensing volume, which is exactly the mechanism an audio brand enters through.

Merchandising growth is attributed principally to the `hololive OFFICIAL CARD GAME` trading card
product, **not** to electronics. Do not read merchandising growth as collab-hardware growth.

**FY2026/3 Q3 cumulative:** revenue JPY 34.6 bn (346億円), +20.2% YoY.
**FY2027/3 Q1:** revenue ~JPY 8.825 bn, −8.3% YoY; merchandising declined on trading-card
release scheduling and difficulties in an EC-operations transition. (Magnitude to re-verify.)

## PART D — Structural findings added by this sweep

1. **Three distinct value chains now identified, not one:**
   - *Audio brand licenses IP/talent* — AVIOT, ONKYO, final, Moondrop. Brand carries product risk.
   - *Music label publishes hardware* — Universal Music / TOoKA BASE. Label carries it, sourcing ODM chassis.
   - *Talent leads development* — Koiwai Kotori × Owltech, funded by crowdfunding. Talent carries creative control.
   The client's entry route determines which of these it is competing against.

2. **Licensing is separately observable only at COVER.** hololive's Licensing/Tie-up line
   (JPY 7.198 bn, +25.3%) is the single public window onto this market's licensing economics.
   ANYCOLOR buries goods in commerce (~70% of revenue) with no licensing split.

3. **Voice-line count spans 8 to 370** across the products found so far (final CyberPatra 8;
   ONKYO 11; Nijisanji CP-TWS01E 13; ag COTSUBU 14; ANIMA Suisei 42; Universal 7 commands +
   15-min drama; AVIOT Kiramune 39; AVIOT Myth 200+; Aogiri 270+; AVIOT Advent 370+).
   It is the industry's own unit of collab depth and it scales with roster size, not price alone.

4. **Retail distribution runs through otaku channels, not audio channels** — Animate Online Shop,
   Tower Records Online, HMV, and ONKYO's own Akihabara anime-audio store (音アニ). e☆イヤホン
   (specialist audio retail) covers the category editorially but the volume channel is anime retail.
