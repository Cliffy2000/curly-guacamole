**Case-study value:** Nikkei xTECH reports the product was rated poorly in internal review before
becoming a 1.6億円 hit — `社内評価ボロボロのイヤホンが一転、1.6億円支援の超人気商品に`.
Directly relevant to a technical team weighing internal-gate risk.
