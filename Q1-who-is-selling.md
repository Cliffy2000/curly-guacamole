# Q1 — WHO IS SELLING COLLABORATION HI-FI HEADPHONES

Japan and China. Compiled 2026-09-07. Every entity below is evidenced by a retrieved source.
Entities searched for and found **not** to participate are listed too, because for an entrant the
absence of a competitor is as material as its presence.

---

## 0. HEADLINE

**23 selling entities confirmed across both markets, in six structurally different roles.**
The market is not one business. An entrant competes against whichever of these six it becomes.

| Role | Who | What they carry |
|---|---|---|
| **Audio brand licenses IP/talent** | AVIOT, ONKYO, final/ag, Moondrop, SONGX, Zeeny, qdc, Hi-Unit | product risk, tooling, acoustics |
| **Major CE brand, occasional entry** | Sony, Victor (JVCKenwood) | brand equity risk, existing platform |
| **Media company / label publishes hardware** | Universal Music (TOoKA BASE), Nippon TV (via final), Taito/Cafereo | IP access, no acoustics capability |
| **Content platform becomes hardware brand** | MissEvan 猫耳FM (bilibili) | audience, app integration |
| **Talent leads development** | Owltech (with Koiwai Kotori) | creative control sits with talent |
| **Software / voice-skin platform** | NAIN (Zeeny Assistant), NAIN's Yv licensing | no per-SKU licensing at all |

**Price span of the confirmed market: JPY 5,980 → JPY 250,000.** A 42× spread. The commonly
cited "collab earphone" price band of ~JPY 20,000 is only the middle of the market.

### 1.4 Zeeny / NAIN — the indie long tail, and the only software-platform answer

**Operating company:** 株式会社ネイン **NAIN Inc.** Founded **2014-11** by 山本健太郎
Yamamoto Kentaro. HQ Shibuya, Tokyo. **~30 staff.** Venture-funded (Nikkei Compass, initial.inc,
Morning Pitch profiles all exist → funding history retrievable).

**Distinct strategic model — this is not a licensing business:**
- **「Zeeny アシスタント」 Zeeny Assistant** — a service that lets the user **re-skin the
  earphone's operation sounds with a chosen character's voice**. Voice skins as a *service*,
  decoupled from the SKU. No per-product IP deal required for the user to get their preferred voice.
- **「Yv」(イヴ)** — NAIN's hearable technology **licensed as software to other audio brands**.
  So NAIN is also a supplier to potential competitors.
- Zeeny Pro Service — voice-driven form entry for non-desk workers (unrelated vertical)

## 6. THE INTERMEDIARY LAYER — the real barrier to entry

From the Toyo Keizai analysis, interviewing Preseed Japan's EVP and Bunka Broadcast Extend's CEO.
**This is the most decision-relevant finding for the client.**

**Rights are fragmented across three separate licensors, each with different industry contact
points:**
1. **Character voice work** — the anime production / rightsholder
2. **The voice actor's personal endorsement** — the talent's own agency
3. **Music-related work** — the label

A collab earphone that uses a character's voice, credits the actor, and carries theme music
therefore requires **three independent negotiations through three unrelated doors**. That, not
tooling or acoustics, is the stated barrier.

**How the incumbents solved it:**
- **AVIOT** — built its own access route by producing radio programming with Bunka Broadcasting
  and running a voice-actor-award-linked show. It manufactures relationships, not just earphones.
- **ONKYO** — owns category retail (Akihabara 音アニ) and partners a specialist for acoustics
  (FitEar) rather than for rights.
- **Universal Music / Nippon TV** — already own the rights side, and buy the hardware in.
- **NAIN (Zeeny)** — sidesteps rights entirely with user-selectable voice skins as a service.
- **TANCHJIM** — sidesteps rights entirely with an in-house original character.

**Market outlook stated in the analysis:** the voice-actor business is 「裾野広げる」 — broadening
its base — with corporate interest driven by talent popularity, individual potential, and social
media response. No revenue, margin, or MOQ figures were disclosed.
Source: https://toyokeizai.net/articles/-/893363?display=b

## 8. SECONDARY MARKET — infrastructure exists

Popular collab models go **out of regular retail after their sales window and rise sharply in
resale value**, per e☆イヤホン's own category commentary. A **dedicated buy-back service for
anime/game/VTuber collab earphones** operates at https://goods-mate.com/earphones/ .
For a made-to-order market this is the only observable secondary price signal, and it is a
candidate proxy for demand where units are undisclosed.
