**Two opposite licensing philosophies, and they matter for deal design:**
- **hololive licenses units.** One SKU carries five talents and 200–370 voice clips. Fewer, bigger deals.

repeatable: Ui supervised both sound and design, and Mea's order window is open right now.

# PART C — CROSS-CUTTING ANALYSIS

## C1. Fourteen partner classes
anime/manga IP · game IP · tokusatsu IP · VTuber unit · VTuber individual · virtual character ·
virtual singer/voicebank · seiyuu individual · seiyuu label or group · musician · utaite ·
radio or TV programme · esports club · other celebrity (comedian) — plus mainstream character
licensors (Sanrio, Disney), **TV announcers** (Universal Music only) and reviewer/influencer
(Crinacle, not talent).

## C2. Five distinct pricing philosophies
1. **Fixed absolute uplift** — AVIOT. +JPY 8,000–12,000 over base, so +25% on flagships and
   +112% on cheap platforms.
2. **Flat collab price** — Universal Music. Most SKUs at exactly the base JPY 14,300, with
   variation driven by character count (JPY 12,100–17,600) rather than by platform.
3. **Near-zero premium on a flagship** — Sony. Value is in engraving, bundling and retail events.
4. **Light uplift on a standing SKU** — Moondrop/SONGX in China. +10%.
5. **Separate premium product class** — ONKYO's FitEar custom IEMs at JPY 104,500–250,000, which
   are not a reskin of anything.

## C3. The merch ecosystem is category-wide
Both AVIOT and ONKYO sell clear files, acrylic stands, badges and wireless chargers against the
same licences. AVIOT goes further, to JPY 33,000 acrylic panels and — the tell — a **JPY 3,850
acrylic earphone stand**, an accessory whose only purpose is displaying the collab earphone.
**The licence is amortised across a goods catalogue and the earphone is treated as a
collectible.** This is how the category works, not one company's quirk.

## C4. Voice content is the real product spec
Range found: 8 clips (final CyberPatra) · 11 words (ONKYO ×3 models) · 13 (ONKYO Kagura Mea,
CP-TWS01E) · 14 (ag COTSUBU) · **15 including hidden commands** (ONKYO Angel Next Door) ·
24 (AVIOT Nakayama Kinni-kun) · 32 in 2 switchable patterns (final TWSN73) · 39 (AVIOT Kiramune,
3 × 13) · 42 (ONKYO Suisei) · 150+ (BUSTAFELLOWS) · 200+ in 10 modes (hololive Myth) ·
220+ (Oshi no Ko) · 270+ in 3 modes (Aogiri) · 360+ across 4 SKUs (Gintama) · 370+ (hololive
Advent, Haikyu!!). Plus **Universal Music bundles a ~15-minute voice drama per actor**, and
ONKYO's GochiUsa model makes the **voice a purchase-time選択** — one of seven characters.

## C5. Channel structure
Brand-direct is dominant (AVIOT ONLINE MALL, ONKYO DIRECT, final store, zeeny.com). Beyond that:
**otaku retail** (Animate, Tower Records, HMV) · **brand-owned physical stores** (ONKYO's 音アニ
in Akihabara, TOoKA BASE Akihabara) · **IP-owner retail** (Disney Store exclusive) ·
**TV shopping** (Nippon TV ×2) · **crowdfunding** (Makuake) · **first-party premium retail**
(Sony Store with events) · **Chinese e-commerce** (JD, Tmall, Pinduoduo, Douyin, Xiaoman app).
Specialist audio retail (e☆イヤホン) covers the category **editorially** but is not the volume channel.
